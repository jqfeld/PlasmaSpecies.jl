PlasmaSpecies.jl

Bookkeeping for the species and reactions of a plasma-chemistry model.

PlasmaSpecies.jl gives one concrete answer to "what is a species, and when are two of them the same" — then builds the hierarchy, reaction parsing and export on top of it. It does no physics: no rate coefficients, no transport, no solver.

What it provides

  • Species — a gas, a charge, and optionally an electronic, vibrational and rotational state. Written in the string notation of the LoKI-B Boltzmann solver ("N2[+,B,vib=3]"), with a p"..." macro for both species and reactions.
  • Gases resolved from chemical formulas — anything built from known nuclides works without registration and knows its own mass, natural-abundance ("CO2") or isotopically specific ("12C-16O2").
  • SpeciesTree — the parent/child hierarchy relating bulk N2 to N2[X] to N2[X,vib=1], with the leaves as the states a model integrates.
  • ReactionFormula / PlasmaReaction — parsed reactions, reversible and fractional coefficients included, and apply_tree to expand one written over lumped species into one per resolved state.
  • to_catalyst — export to Catalyst.jl reaction networks, via a package extension.

Installation

julia> import Pkg; Pkg.add(url="https://github.com/jqfeld/PlasmaSpecies.jl.git")

A first look

julia> using PlasmaSpecies
julia> sp = p"N2[+,B]"N2[+,B]
julia> gas(sp), charge(sp), electronic_state(sp)(N₂, +, B)
julia> mass(sp)4.6517430473362253e-26
julia> r = p"e + N2 --> 2e + N2[+]"e+N2-->2e+N2[+]
julia> t = SpeciesTree(["e", "N2[X,vib=0]", "N2[X,vib=1]", "N2[+]"])nothing
├─ e
├─ N2
│  └─ N2[X]
│     ├─ N2[X,vib=0]
│     └─ N2[X,vib=1]
└─ N2[+]
julia> leaves(t)4-element Vector{Species{S, C, E, V, Nothing, Nothing, Nothing, Nothing} where {S, C<:Charge, E, V}}:
 e
 N2[X,vib=0]
 N2[X,vib=1]
 N2[+]
julia> apply_tree(t, p"e + N2[X] --> 2e + N2[+]")2-element Vector{Tuple{Float64, ReactionFormula}}:

 (1.0, e+N2[X,vib=1]-->2e+N2[+])
